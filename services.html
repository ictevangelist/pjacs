One trusted craftsman across your whole project — design, build, fit and finish. Here’s what PJA Carpentry & Joinery can do for your home.

Bespoke Kitchens

The kitchen your house was waiting for

Paul has fitted kitchens for some of Gloucestershire’s most prestigious kitchen companies — now he brings that same showroom standard directly to you, without the showroom mark-up. Design, supply and installation, or installation only: it’s your call.

  • Free design consultation with photorealistic CAD visuals
  • Full design, supply & install — or fit-only for your own kitchen
  • In-frame, shaker, painted and contemporary styles
  • Worktops, appliances, tiling and lighting coordinated in one project
  • Clever refurbishments too — we’ve transformed tired kitchens without replacing them

Fitted Bedrooms & Wardrobes

Storage that fits your life — and your walls

Made-to-measure wardrobes, dressing rooms and bedroom furniture that use every awkward corner, sloping ceiling and alcove. Supplied from quality manufacturers such as Symphony, JJO and Crown Imperial — or built fully bespoke by hand.

  • Floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes & walk-in dressing rooms
  • Under-eaves and loft-room storage solutions
  • Home offices and studies, made to measure
  • Options for every budget, from supply-and-fit to fully bespoke

Luxury Bathrooms

Bathrooms, beautifully coordinated

A great bathroom is a dozen trades meeting perfectly. With PJA, one person owns the whole picture — design, carpentry, tiling, plumbing coordination and finishing — so the details line up and the project runs to plan.

  • Complete bathroom design & installation
  • 3D design & planning before work begins
  • Tiling, flooring, bespoke vanity units & panelling
  • One point of contact from first sketch to final seal
