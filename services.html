Bespoke Kitchens

The kitchen your house was waiting for

This is the heart of the business. Paul has fitted kitchens for some of Gloucestershire’s most prestigious kitchen companies, and he brings that showroom standard straight to you, without the showroom mark-up. From in-frame classics to sleek contemporary spaces, every kitchen is designed around how you live and fitted by hand.

  • Photorealistic CAD visuals, so you can see the design before it’s built
  • Full design, supply and install, or fit-only if you’ve bought your own kitchen
  • In-frame, shaker, painted and contemporary styles
  • Worktops, appliances, tiling and lighting coordinated in one project
  • Larger schemes welcome, including open-plan conversions and structural alterations
