Cookie Policy

What this site stores on your device, what it’s for, and how to change your mind.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

This site sets no cookies at all unless you accept analytics in the cookie banner. If you choose “Essential only”, the only thing stored is your choice itself.

Changing your mind

Click Cookie Settings (also in the footer of every page) to reopen the banner and change your choice. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings. See your browser’s help pages for instructions.

More detail

Analytics data is collected with IP anonymisation and used only in aggregate, to understand which pages are useful and how people find us. For how this fits into the bigger picture, see our Privacy Policy.
