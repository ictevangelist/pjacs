About

Meet Paul Anderson.

PJA Carpentry Joinery Services is Paul Anderson, a time-served carpenter and joiner with more than 30 years in the trade and a reputation across Gloucestershire built on word of mouth.

Family run since 2010£2m public liability insuranceFree quotations

Trained where standards are highest

Before founding PJA, Paul learned his trade with some of Gloucestershire’s most respected kitchen companies, fitting kitchens, bedrooms and bathrooms for the Gloucester Kitchen Centre and working as a kitchen fitter for Cotswold Kitchens of Nailsworth. He also spent years as a carpenter and joiner with national housebuilder David Wilson Homes.

That background shows in the work: showroom-standard fitting, tidy and disciplined sites, and a good eye for design from years spent around premium kitchens and interiors.

Since 2010, PJA has been a family-run business serving homeowners who want things done properly, including high-end builds where the details matter and the clients expect nothing less.
