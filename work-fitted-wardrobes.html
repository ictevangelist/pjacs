Fitted wardrobes with mirror

Floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes in a warm light oak finish, with a full-length mirror door built into the run. Every centimetre from carpet to ceiling works as storage, with no dusty void on top and no wasted gap at the skirting.

The doors were hung and aligned by hand so the grain runs consistently across the whole face, and the mirror sits flush in its frame rather than stuck on as an afterthought.

Fitted bedroom furniture is measured for the room it lives in. That is the whole point, and it is why it beats freestanding furniture every single time.

The details

  • Floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes
  • Integrated full-length mirror door
  • Light oak finish with consistent grain
  • Built to the room's exact dimensions
