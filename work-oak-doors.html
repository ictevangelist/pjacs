Oak internal doors

Sometimes the job is simply doors done right. This pair of oak internal doors was hung, eased and finished so they close with a soft click and sit dead level in their frames, with even margins all the way round.

New doors transform a hallway more than almost anything else you can spend the money on, and hanging them well is a genuine skill: an oak door is heavy, and a lazy fit shows forever.

Part of PJA's maintenance and carpentry service: the smaller jobs, done to the same standard as the big ones.
